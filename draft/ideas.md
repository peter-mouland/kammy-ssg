# Feature Ideas

## Team of the Week
Show the best-performing player at each position for a given gameweek, displayed on a football pitch graphic. Uses custom scoring rules to determine the top player per position (GK, CB, FB, MID, WA, CA).

## Free Agent Gems
Highlight unowned players who scored the most points in a gameweek. Helps managers spot potential transfers they might be missing.

## Bench Pain Index
Calculate how many points each manager left on the bench (sub slot). Rank managers by wasted bench potential across the season.

## Manager Points Over Time
Line chart showing each manager's cumulative season points over gameweeks. Visualise form streaks, comebacks, and collapses.

## Position Battles
Compare two managers head-to-head across each position slot. Show who wins each position matchup and by how much.

## Player Form
Rolling 5-gameweek form rating for each player. Surface trending players who are picking up or dropping off.

## Optimal Team Comparison
For each gameweek, calculate the best possible team a manager could have fielded from their roster. Compare actual points vs optimal points to show decision-making quality.
